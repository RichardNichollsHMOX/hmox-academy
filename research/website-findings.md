## Sales and auctions
- HMO X Sales, formerly HMO Sales Expert; 0% buyer commission; 700+ sales since 2018; 150+ valuations a month.
- Committed Sale: £3,500 reservation deposit, 56-day exclusivity, AML/ID/proof of funds and solicitor before exclusivity; 90 to 180 days to exchange. No fee or minimum published.
- Auctions: guide 20 to 25% below open-market value; reserve within 10% of guide, undisclosed, locked once registered bidding can be read; 6 to 8 week completion; 100% of auction sales completed, 90% of lots to live bidding sell, 58% exchange before auction day; launched June 2025.
